## Lines to avoid

- “DISP-001 automatically refunds S$3.44.” Its local policy baseline is **human review, S$0 pending**.
- “DISP-002 upholds the fee in the app.” It is absent from the case selector, and its local policy baseline is **human review** because a fraud flag raises the confidence requirement.
- “The three cards show each agent’s report.” They are role descriptions plus a Judge summary; separate advocate reports are not returned to Streamlit.
- “We sent the messages / paid the refund / verified a Ryde production policy.” The app proposes messages and amounts using organiser supplied hackathon policy and fixtures.
- “The confidence is calibrated accuracy.” It is a model self assessment or local heuristic.
